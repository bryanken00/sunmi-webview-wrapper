Text size 32 columns

Characters per line. Fewer columns means bigger letters, and big letters print far more reliably than small ones.

Stroke — vertical stems 0.5 dots
Stroke — horizontal bars 0.5 dots

Thickens the strokes by redrawing them offset. The two axes fix different things: the first widens upright stems like the sides of an H, the second the flat bars like its crossbar and the waist of an e. If letters look half-there, it is usually the horizontal one.

Darkness 200

How much grey counts as a dot to burn. Higher keeps more of each anti-aliased edge, so text gets fatter and darker; too high and the holes in e, a and o fill in.

Label pitch 33.0 mm
264 dots

Top of one sticker to the top of the next — the label plus the gap. With no gap sensor this is what keeps successive labels lined up, so measure it on your roll and fine-tune with the calibration strip.

Prints three stickers with a bar at the top and bottom edge of each. If the bars sit on the edges of all three, the pitch is right; if they creep up the pitch is short, if they creep down it is long.
